Privacy policy

Game: AETHRION · Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Versão em português

This policy explains how AETHRION, a game by Tower Light Games, handles your information. In short: the game does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data.

1. Data we collect

None. AETHRION does not ask for an account, name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data. The game has no ads and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the game stores only on your own phone your progress (saved game), the language you chose, your sound setting and whether you have finished the tutorial. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

3. In-game purchases

The full version of the game is unlocked through a purchase made on Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store your card or account details. The game only receives confirmation from the store that the purchase was made, in order to unlock the content.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. Children

AETHRION can be played by people of all ages. Because the game collects no personal data, it collects no data from children either.

5. Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to know what personal data is processed and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since we do not collect personal data, we hold no data about you. If you have any questions, contact us at the email below.

6. Changes to this policy

If the game ever handles data differently, this page will be updated before the change, with the new date at the top.
